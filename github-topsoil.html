Mini topsoil calculator

Measure the bed in feet, and the depth in inches. The calculator works out cubic feet and cubic yards.

Cubic yards equals length times width times depth in inches, divided by 324. A 4 by 8 foot bed at 6 inches deep needs about 0.59 cubic yards, so order 1 cubic yard. Order about a tenth extra, since soil settles. Measure depth in five or six places and use the average. Full calculator with measuring steps: topsoilcalconline.com.
